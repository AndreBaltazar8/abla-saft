# Sources

All were downloaded on 2026-10-31. `tools/sources.txt` pins their URLs and SHA-256 hashes, and
`make sources` fetches them into `.sources/`.

## Why they aren't vendored

The plan was to vendor the XSD and the Portaria. AT's pages link no terms of use that were
found to permit redistribution, and the XSD's own metadata says "Copyright OECD" with AT as
author, with no licence. So nothing from AT is in this public repository: a hash-pinned
download gives the same exact files, and a replaced file fails loudly. This repository
holds only what's derived from them as facts: field limits, and the country and currency
lists in `src/codes.ab`, generated by `tools/gen-codes.py`.

| Short name | What | Read? |
|---|---|---|
| XSD | `saftpt1.04_01.xsd`, ModificationDate 2022-05-17, linked from AT's SAF-T (PT) page | Whole, by a structure dump and all 19 assertions; used by the validators |
| Demo | `saft_idemo599999999.xml`, AT's demonstration file | Used in tests; not read line by line |
| P302 | Portaria 302/2016, AT's consolidated PDF, Annex I | Generic aspects, table 1, 4.1 and 4.4 in full. Tables 2.2, 2.4, 2.5, 4.2 and 4.3 in the parts on control totals, status, Hash and types, not line by line. Tables 2.1 and 3 and the taxonomies, not read |
| P363 | Portaria 363/2010, AT's consolidated PDF | Whole. Its field numbers are older than P302's |
| P195 | Portaria 195/2020, AT's PDF | Whole |
| D8632 | Despacho 8632/2014, **AT's unofficial English translation** | Whole. The Portuguese original wasn't obtained |
| QR | "Especificações Técnicas, Código QR", version 1.1, October 2020, in English; Portuguese PDF also pinned | English in full; Portuguese only opened |

Not read: Diário da República originals (its site gave the tools no text), Declaração de
Retificação 2-A/2017, Decreto-Lei 28/2019, Portaria 321-A/2007, the "Manual de Integração de
Software", and AT's FAQs (404 when fetched; faqs-00276 and faqs-00879 seen only as search
summaries). Nothing in the code relies on them, apart from what's flagged in the README.

## Where the sources disagree

- **HashControl "Não certificado".** D8632 2.3.4 f) gives it for integrating an uncertified
  program's documents; the XSD's pattern rejects it. P302 4.1.4.5 says "0". The check follows
  the XSD.
- **Field limits.** P302 gives 8 for the header's PostalCode and 20 for ProductCompanyTaxID;
  the XSD allows 20 and 30. The check follows the XSD, since it's what files are validated with.
- **QR character counts.** QR's figures 6 and 7 count 129 and 150, including stray spaces after
  "G:" and "H:" in its printed messages, against its rule 3 a). Without them they are 127 and
  149; the tests use those.
- **P363's field numbers** predate P302's (4.1.4.6 there is InvoiceDate; 4.1.4.7 here). The
  fields are named, so the meaning is clear.
- **ATCUD "0".** P302 says to write 0 "until regulated", and P195 has since regulated it. So 0 is
  a warning, still accepted because the XSD and older documents have it.
